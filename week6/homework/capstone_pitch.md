# Capstone Pitch: Family Child Care Listing and Price Sheet Assistant

California has about 14,000 licensed family child care homes, but parents cannot easily see which ones have openings, what they cost, or what languages they speak, because the state's public data only lists name, city, ZIP code, and capacity. I will build an LLM chat assistant, embedded by iframe on MyTeachers123.com, that asks a provider four required questions (CDSS facility number, email, children enrolled now as infants 0-24 months, toddlers 2-5 years, and school-age, and prices by class) plus her expected seat changes over the next six months. It computes open seats from the CDSS licensed capacity and the CDSS infant limits (22 CCR 102416.5), and it generates a web price sheet with a QR code linking to her MyTeachers123 page, a claim form, and a parent-facing description. ZIP-code Census data is used to suggest services and funding programs she may qualify for. The architecture is FastAPI plus LangChain with `ChatOpenAI` (gpt-5.4-mini), `PydanticOutputParser`, and `OutputFixingParser`. A deterministic state machine controls the conversation, and the LLM only extracts answers and writes text. Pydantic validators enforce the business rules: infant limits by capacity, dates within six months, plausible prices per unit, and only curated programs. This extends Week 6 (prompt engineering, structured output, and LangChain with Pydantic validation and retry). I will evaluate it with the Week 6 method on real provider messages and flyers: extraction correctness, validator catch rate, and tokens and cost per completed listing.
